We Specialize in Hard-to-Find Construction Equipment Parts

Most parts calls start the same way: a machine is down, the dealer quotes six weeks, and the schedule does not care. Since 1994 we have supplied construction and heavy machinery parts to contractors, municipalities, rental fleets and owner-operators across New Jersey and well beyond — new, used, refurbished, or built to match the piece that failed.

We are a non-OEM supplier by choice. Aftermarket and good used components are usually the faster, cheaper route to a running machine, and on older iron they are often the only route. Our job is to tell you honestly which route fits your machine and budget, then get the part in your hands. Here is the full OEM-versus-aftermarket comparison.

Thirty years buys a network you cannot shortcut — salvage yards, rebuilders, machine shops, specialists still holding shelf stock for machines the manufacturer dropped a decade ago. That network is the difference between "discontinued" and "here Thursday." Then we move it: same-day and next-day delivery across New Jersey and the surrounding region.

Start with numbers — make, year and serial; engine model and AR or CPL number if you have them; what failed. Send them through the part request form or call (908) 200-7199. A person reads it and a person answers. See everything we handle on the services page.
