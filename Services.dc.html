Hard-to-Find & Obsolete Parts Sourcing

This is what DEP is for. When the dealer network returns "no longer available," we go to the places no catalog lists: salvage operations, rebuilders, regional dead stock, and machinists who can reproduce a legacy component. Obsolete parts are found, not ordered.

Send whatever identifiers you have — a machine serial, an AR or CPL number, even a clear photo of the failed piece is enough to start.

How It Works

Four steps, and only the first one is yours.

  1. 01 Send your numbers Make, year, serial, engine model and AR/CPL — whatever is on the plate.
  2. 02 We source it New, used, refurbished or built — we work the network until there is an answer.
  3. 03 You approve the quote Condition, price and lead time in plain language. Financing if you want it.
  4. 04 We deliver Same-day or next-day where we can reach you; shipped where we cannot.
