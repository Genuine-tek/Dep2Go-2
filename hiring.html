The Role

We are looking for a Heavy Machinery Parts Manager to oversee operations for an active New Jersey-based heavy machinery equipment and parts vendor. This is the hub role: quotes go out through you, orders come back through you, and the deliveries that keep customers' machines running get coordinated by you.

It suits someone organized and unbothered by a busy phone — comfortable talking to a contractor, a driver, and a vendor inside the same ten minutes, and precise enough to keep the paperwork straight behind it.

Responsibilities

  • 01Prepare customer quotes and process sales orders
  • 02Create and manage invoices and purchase orders
  • 03Coordinate parts deliveries and pickups
  • 04Maintain organized financial and transaction records
  • 05Communicate professionally with customers, vendors, drivers and technicians

Qualifications

  • Valid driver's license — required
  • Experience preparing quotes, invoices and purchase orders
  • Accounting or bookkeeping skills highly preferred
  • Basic computer and data-entry skills
