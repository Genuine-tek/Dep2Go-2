We Specialize in Hard to Find Construction Equipment Parts

Most parts calls open the same way. A machine is down, the dealer quotes six weeks, and the schedule does not care. Since 1994 we have supplied construction and heavy machinery parts to contractors, municipalities, rental fleets and owner operators across New Jersey and far beyond: used, remanufactured, or fabricated to match the piece that failed.

Parts have also become punishingly expensive. Producer prices for construction machinery parts sold separately have more than doubled since January 2020, climbing from an index of 152.1 to 316.8 by August 2026, according to U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics data. That is the arithmetic behind every quote you have winced at lately, and it is precisely why a credible non O.E.M. route is worth having.

We are a non O.E.M. supplier by choice. Aftermarket and sound used components are usually the quicker and cheaper path to a running machine, and on older iron they are frequently the only path. Our job is to say plainly which route suits your machine and your budget, then put the part in your hands. Here is the full O.E.M. versus aftermarket comparison.

Thirty years buys a network you cannot shortcut: salvage yards, rebuilders, machine shops, and specialists still holding shelf stock for models the manufacturer abandoned a decade ago. That network is the whole difference between “discontinued” and “here Thursday.” Then we move it, with same day and next day pickup and delivery across New Jersey, New York, Pennsylvania, and Connecticut.

Start with numbers. Make, year and serial; engine model, engine serial and the AR or CPL number if you have them; and what failed. Send them through the part request form or call (973) 332-8598. A person reads it and a person answers. See everything we handle on the services page.

Common Questions About Construction Equipment Parts

Does Direct Equipment Parts sell O.E.M. parts or aftermarket parts?

We are an independent non O.E.M. supplier. We source aftermarket, used and remanufactured construction equipment parts, and we fabricate or adapt components when nothing off the shelf fits. On discontinued machines an aftermarket or good used part is often the only route to a running machine, and we will tell you plainly which route suits your machine and budget.

How quickly can I get a construction equipment part?

Same day and next day heavy equipment parts delivery is available across New Jersey, New York, Pennsylvania, and Connecticut. How fast depends on where the part sits rather than how far you are from us, which is why we ask for machine and engine numbers up front.

Can you find parts for a discontinued or obsolete machine?

Yes. Obsolete and hard to find parts are the core of what we do. Since 1994 we have built a network of salvage yards, rebuilders, machine shops and specialists still holding shelf stock for models the manufacturer stopped supporting years ago.

What information do you need to find my part?

Machine make, model, year and serial number; engine model, engine serial and the AR or CPL number if you have them; and a short description of what failed. The more numbers you send, the faster the search closes.

Which areas does Direct Equipment Parts serve?

We are a heavy equipment parts supplier based in Clinton, New Jersey, delivering across New Jersey, New York, Pennsylvania, and Connecticut. Parts ship nationwide.

What happens if you cannot find the part?

Our consulting service carries a no solution, no charge guarantee. If we cannot solve the problem, you owe nothing.
